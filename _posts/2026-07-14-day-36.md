what_i_learned: |
   Today was dedicated to two major parts of our Alzheimer's disease research project: preparing our final presentation through a short commercial-style
   video and writing the literature review for my assigned target, BACE1. Both activities helped me understand that successful research is not only about
   developing machine learning models but also about communicating scientific ideas in a way that everyone can understand.

   During the first half of the day, our team planned and developed an Alzheimer's awareness commercial that demonstrates how artificial intelligence can
   support drug discovery. Instead of creating a traditional presentation, we designed a story that follows an Alzheimer's patient, researchers, and a
   news reporter to explain the real-world challenges faced by patients and how AI can help researchers identify promising drug candidates more efficiently.
   We discussed the storyline, assigned acting roles to each team member, revised the dialogue several times, and worked on making the presentation more
   engaging by adding humor, emotional moments, and smooth scene transitions. We also focused on keeping the scientific information accurate while making
   the story simple enough for a general audience to understand.

   Through this process, I learned how visual storytelling can make complex scientific concepts much easier to communicate. Rather than explaining machine
   learning algorithms with technical terminology, we showed how AI helps researchers reduce the number of compounds that need to be tested, allowing them
   to focus on the most promising drug candidates first. This experience improved my understanding of science communication and demonstrated how creativity
   can make research presentations more memorable and impactful.

   During the second half of the day, I concentrated on writing the literature review for my assigned Alzheimer's disease target, BACE1 (Beta-site Amyloid
   Precursor Protein Cleaving Enzyme 1). I carefully reviewed published research papers to better understand the biological role of BACE1 and why it is
   considered one of the most important therapeutic targets in Alzheimer's disease. I learned that BACE1 initiates the cleavage of amyloid precursor protein
   (APP), producing amyloid-beta peptides that can accumulate and form plaques in the brain, one of the major pathological hallmarks of Alzheimer's disease.
   Understanding this biological pathway helped me appreciate why inhibiting BACE1 has been an important focus of Alzheimer's drug discovery research.

   While preparing the literature review, I summarized the findings from multiple research papers, organized the information into a logical structure, and
   explained why our project focuses on identifying new BACE1 inhibitors using artificial intelligence and machine learning. I also learned the importance
   of supporting every scientific statement with reliable evidence and properly cited research articles, which is essential for writing a high-quality
   scientific paper.

blockers: |
   Developing a commercial that was both scientifically accurate and entertaining required multiple revisions. Our team needed to carefully balance humor,
   emotional storytelling, and educational content without reducing the seriousness of Alzheimer's disease. We also had to ensure that the commercial fit
   within the required presentation time while clearly explaining our research objectives.

   Writing the literature review was also time-consuming because it required reading several scientific papers, comparing their findings, verifying the
   biological information about BACE1, and organizing the content into a clear and logical scientific narrative. Ensuring that all references were accurate
   and that every statement was supported by published research required careful attention to detail.

reflection: |
   Today helped me realize that research extends far beyond data analysis and machine learning model development. An effective researcher must also be able
   to communicate scientific discoveries clearly to both technical and non-technical audiences. Creating the Alzheimer's awareness commercial challenged me
   to transform complex AI concepts into a simple and engaging story, while writing the BACE1 literature review strengthened my understanding of the target's
   biological importance and the scientific foundation of our project.

   Overall, today's work improved both my scientific writing skills and my presentation skills. It also reinforced the importance of combining technical
   knowledge, creativity, teamwork, and effective communication to make scientific research meaningful and accessible to others. These experiences will be
   valuable as we continue preparing our final research presentation and manuscript.
