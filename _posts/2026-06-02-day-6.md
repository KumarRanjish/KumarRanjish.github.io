what_i_learned: |
   Today, we had a meeting with our research mentor and discussed the research papers that were assigned to us. Each team member shared the information they learned     from their paper, which helped us better understand our project and different research approaches.

   After that, we learned about how to write and revise a research paper. The session was very helpful because it explained the structure of a research paper and        provided useful tips for improving our writing and preparing journal-quality work.

   Later, we attended a session about Amazon Prime and how live game streaming works. We learned about the technologies involved in streaming, including buffering,      redundancy, Content Delivery Networks (CDNs), and how data traffic is managed to ensure a smooth viewing experience for users.

   We also participated in a Kahoot quiz, which was one of my favorite activities of the day. The quiz made learning more interactive and helped reinforce the           concepts we had learned during the sessions.

   In the Python session, we continued working with the Groq API and practiced building AI-powered applications. We created two games using Groq: a Trivia Game and a    Riddle Master Game. It was exciting to see how AI could be integrated into interactive applications, and building the games helped strengthen my understanding of     Python, Gradio, and API usage.

   Overall, today was both educational and enjoyable. I learned more about research writing, live streaming technologies, and AI application development while           gaining valuable hands-on experience.
