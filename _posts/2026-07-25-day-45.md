what_i_learned: |
   Today marked the first day of the Summer AI Research Symposium, where I had the
   opportunity to present the research that I had been working on throughout the
   internship. During the poster presentation, I explained our project on AI-driven
   drug discovery for Alzheimer's disease to faculty members, mentors, researchers,
   students, and symposium visitors. Presenting the complete research workflow—from
   data preprocessing and QSAR modeling to candidate selection and molecular
   docking—helped me strengthen my understanding of the project while improving my
   confidence in communicating technical concepts to audiences with different levels
   of scientific knowledge.

   Throughout the poster session, I answered questions about our machine learning
   models, descriptor generation, molecular docking workflow, and the significance of
   using artificial intelligence to accelerate Alzheimer's drug discovery. These
   discussions helped me realize the importance of clearly explaining complex
   scientific concepts in a simple and organized manner while supporting every
   explanation with evidence from our research.

   In addition to the poster presentation, I participated in the K-12 Hackathon with
   my teammates. Together, we brainstormed ideas, designed an AI-based solution, and
   developed our project within a limited amount of time. The hackathon challenged us
   to think critically, divide responsibilities efficiently, and collaborate under
   pressure while applying the technical and problem-solving skills we had developed
   throughout the internship.

   I also explored many other research posters presented by fellow interns and
   learned about a wide variety of artificial intelligence applications in different
   scientific disciplines. Observing how other researchers approached their problems,
   communicated their findings, and designed their posters provided valuable insights
   into effective scientific presentation and interdisciplinary research.

blockers: |
   Presenting the research poster to many different visitors required adapting my
   explanations based on each audience's technical background. Some attendees were
   familiar with artificial intelligence, while others were not, so I needed to
   explain complex concepts using clear and accessible language without losing the
   scientific meaning.

   During the hackathon, our team worked within strict time constraints while making
   important design decisions. Managing time effectively, coordinating ideas, and
   ensuring that every team member contributed efficiently required continuous
   communication and teamwork throughout the event.

reflection: |
   Today was one of the most memorable experiences of the internship because it gave
   me the opportunity to showcase months of research while interacting with faculty,
   researchers, mentors, and visitors. The poster presentation strengthened my
   confidence in scientific communication, while the hackathon reinforced the value
   of teamwork, creativity, and rapid problem-solving.

   Overall, today's experiences demonstrated that successful research is not only
   about developing accurate AI models but also about effectively communicating
   scientific discoveries, collaborating with others, and applying knowledge to solve
   meaningful real-world problems. It was an inspiring day that highlighted both the
   technical and professional skills developed throughout the Summer AI Research
   Institute.
