what_i_learned: |
   Today I continued working on the external validation stage of my BACE1 QSAR project. I compared my cleaned internal validation dataset with a newly collected external BACE1 dataset to ensure that there were no overlapping compounds between the two datasets. This step is essential because external validation should always be performed using completely unseen compounds to accurately evaluate the performance of the machine learning models.

   After removing the overlapping compounds, I reviewed the external dataset to identify the important columns required for validation and checked whether any unnecessary or empty columns should be removed. I also verified that the dataset was suitable for external validation before moving to the next stage of the project.

   During the data preparation process, I found that the initial subset of compounds belonged to only one activity class. To solve this, I worked on preparing a balanced dataset containing both active and inactive compounds. This helped me better understand the importance of maintaining class balance when evaluating classification models.

   In addition to the dataset preparation, I started preparing the presentation script for our final research presentation. I organized the complete workflow into a logical sequence, beginning with dataset collection, data cleaning, quality control, QSAR model development, internal validation, external validation, and the overall significance of the results. I also learned how to explain each step in simple and easy-to-understand language so the audience can clearly follow the entire research process.

blockers: |
   The main challenge today was ensuring that the external validation dataset contained only unique compounds that were not present in the internal validation dataset. Another difficulty was obtaining a balanced set of active and inactive compounds because the first selected compounds belonged to only one class. Preparing the presentation script also required organizing complex technical concepts into a clear and natural flow.

reflection: |
   Today's work reinforced the importance of careful dataset preparation before performing external validation. I learned that preventing data leakage and maintaining balanced classes are essential for producing reliable and trustworthy machine learning results. Preparing the presentation script also improved my understanding of the overall research workflow, allowing me to confidently explain each stage of the project from data collection to model validation. Overall, today's progress moved me one step closer to completing both the experimental work and the final presentation.
