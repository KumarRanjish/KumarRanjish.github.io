what_i_learned: |
   Today, our team delivered our research presentation to our mentors and received
   valuable feedback on both the technical content and the overall presentation.
   The feedback helped us identify areas where the slides could be improved,
   including simplifying complex information, improving the visual layout,
   increasing text readability, and ensuring that our research workflow was easy
   for the audience to follow.

   After receiving the feedback, we carefully revised each slide by making the
   recommended changes. We reorganized sections, enlarged important text,
   improved the design, removed unnecessary elements, and refined the content so
   that every slide clearly communicated the objectives, methodology, and results
   of our AI-driven Alzheimer's disease drug discovery project.

   Once the presentation was updated, we spent the rest of the day practicing as
   a team. We rehearsed multiple times to improve our confidence, maintain smooth
   transitions between presenters, and explain each stage of our research—from
   data preprocessing and QSAR modeling to candidate selection and molecular
   docking—in a clear, professional, and engaging manner.

reflection: |
   Today reinforced the importance of constructive feedback in both research and
   scientific communication. I learned that even a strong presentation can become
   much more effective through continuous refinement and practice. Revising our
   slides and rehearsing together increased my confidence, strengthened my public
   speaking skills, and helped me better understand how to communicate complex
   research in a simple and organized way. I now feel well prepared and excited
   for our final presentation.
