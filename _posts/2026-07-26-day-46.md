   Today marked the final day of the Summer AI Research Symposium and the culmination
   of nine weeks of research, learning, and collaboration. My team delivered our final
   presentation on AI-driven drug discovery for Alzheimer's disease, where we presented

   molecular descriptor generation, QSAR modeling, candidate selection, and molecular
   docking. Presenting the project to faculty members, mentors, researchers, and fellow
   participants strengthened my confidence in explaining both the scientific and
   technical aspects of our work while answering questions from the audience.

   clear, organized, and engaging manner. Rather than only focusing on technical
   results, we emphasized the real-world impact of using artificial intelligence to
   accelerate the discovery of potential Alzheimer's drug candidates. This experience
   demonstrated how effective scientific communication can make complex research
   understandable to audiences with different backgrounds.

   First Place in the K-12 Hackathon. This achievement recognized the innovation,
   collaboration, and dedication our team demonstrated throughout the competition.
   I was also honored to receive the Second Place Best Spotlight Award for creating
   and presenting our research video. Receiving these awards was an unforgettable
   experience and reflected the hard work, creativity, and commitment that we invested
   throughout the internship.

   Looking back on the entire Summer AI Research Institute, I realized how much I
   developed in machine learning, QSAR modeling, molecular docking, scientific
   communication, teamwork, and research methodology. The symposium served as the
   perfect conclusion to an incredible learning journey and reinforced my motivation
   to continue pursuing research in artificial intelligence for healthcare.
