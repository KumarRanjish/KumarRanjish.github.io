## What I Learned

Today was a very productive and fast-paced day in the SAIRI program. We started by meeting with our mentors and faculty members, which gave us the opportunity to connect with experienced professionals and learn more about the goals of the program. It was helpful to hear their advice and understand how they will support us throughout our research journey.

One of the main topics we covered was Figma. I learned how designers use Figma to create user interfaces and mobile app prototypes before actual development begins. We discussed important design concepts such as User Interface (UI), User Experience (UX), wireframes, layouts, and hex color codes. I found it interesting how much planning and creativity go into designing an application before writing any code. Learning about prototypes also helped me understand how ideas can be visualized and tested before implementation.

Later, we moved on to Python 102. During this session, I learned more about functions, parameters, loops, and object-oriented programming concepts such as classes and objects. I gained a better understanding of how functions help organize code and make programs more reusable. The discussion about classes showed me how programmers can model real-world objects and behaviors in software. We also explored examples that demonstrated how these concepts are used in actual applications.

What I enjoyed most about today's learning experience was the way the instructor explained complex concepts in a simple and engaging manner. The instructor was very knowledgeable and interacted well with students, making the session enjoyable and easy to follow. Even though the day moved quickly and covered many topics, I was able to learn several new concepts related to both software design and programming.

Overall, today's activities helped me strengthen my understanding of app design and Python programming. I am becoming more comfortable with the tools and concepts being introduced, and I look forward to applying these skills in future projects and research activities throughout the program.
