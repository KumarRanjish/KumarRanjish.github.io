what_i_learned: |
   Today was one of the biggest milestones of my research journey. I successfully completed
   the complete molecular docking workflow for my BACE1 Alzheimer's disease project. I learned
   how to prepare the protein structure, prepare ligand molecules, and understand the complete
   molecular docking process from start to finish.

   I also learned how different computational tools work together in a drug discovery pipeline.
   I understood how the BACE1 protein structure (PDB ID: 7E3H) was downloaded from the Protein
   Data Bank, cleaned in ChimeraX, and prepared for docking. I also learned how ligand compounds
   are obtained from PubChem, how molecular descriptors are generated using RDKit in Google Colab,
   and how the best compounds predicted by the QSAR model are selected for docking studies.

   Another important achievement today was documenting the entire molecular docking workflow
   step by step, from downloading the protein to preparing the final docking candidates. This
   helped me understand not only how to perform molecular docking but also why each step is
   important in AI-driven drug discovery for Alzheimer's disease.
